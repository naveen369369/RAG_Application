# Customer Escalation & Complaint Resolution — Customer Help Center

## Overview

When a standard support interaction does not resolve your issue, our escalation process ensures your concern is routed to the right team at the right level. Every complaint is logged in our system, assigned a case ID, and tracked until it is fully closed. We are committed to defined response times at every stage and to delivering a documented outcome for every escalation.

---

## Section 1: When an Escalation Is Needed

Not every issue requires escalation. Standard support handles most questions effectively in a single interaction. However, you should request an escalation when:

- A refund has not been received more than 10 business days after approval.
- The same issue has been reported to support more than twice without resolution.
- A support agent provided incorrect information that caused a financial or service loss.
- Your account was suspended or deactivated without any prior notice or explanation.
- You have a confirmed or suspected data breach or privacy violation — this requires immediate escalation.
- You experienced unprofessional behavior or misconduct from a support agent.

For routine billing questions, technical issues resolved in one session, or general product questions, standard support is the appropriate channel.

---

## Section 2: Escalation Tiers

Our escalation structure has four levels, each with a defined owner, trigger condition, and response commitment.

Tier 1 — Frontline Support (Customer Service Agent): Handles first contact, standard and routine issues. SLA: Real-time or within 4 hours.
Tier 2 — Senior Agent Review (Senior CSR / Team Lead): Handles unresolved issues after Tier 1. SLA: 4–8 business hours.
Tier 3 — Specialist Resolution (Department Specialist): Handles complex or cross-functional issues. SLA: 1–2 business days.
Tier 4 — Executive Escalation (Customer Experience Manager): Handles formal complaints, legal concerns, or repeated failures. SLA: 2–5 business days.

### How to Request an Escalation
During a Live Chat or phone call, say clearly: **"I would like to escalate this to a senior agent."** The agent is required to transfer your case or arrange a callback from a team lead.

By email, reply to your open support ticket and include the phrase **ESCALATION REQUEST** in the subject line. This automatically flags the case for priority review.

Through the Help Center portal, go to **My Cases > Open Case > Request Escalation** and submit the form. You will receive a confirmation email with your escalation case ID within 15 minutes.

## Section 3: Complaint Categories and Routing

Different complaint types are routed to different specialist teams. Knowing where your complaint belongs helps set the right expectations for resolution time.

- **Billing overcharge or unauthorized transaction** — routed to the Billing Specialist team. High priority.
- **Product defect or safety concern** — routed to the Product Quality team. Critical priority.
- **Delivery failure or lost package** — routed to the Logistics team. High priority.
- **Agent misconduct or poor service experience** — routed to Quality Assurance for review. Medium priority.
- **Privacy violation or data breach** — routed directly to the Data Protection Officer. Critical priority.
- **Account suspension without notice** — routed to the Trust and Safety team. High priority.
- **Refund denied despite policy compliance** — routed to a Senior Agent or Manager. High priority.
- **Service not matching advertised terms** — routed to the Legal and Compliance team. High priority.

---

## Section 4: How a Complaint Is Resolved

Every formal complaint follows the same seven-step process from submission to closure.

1. **Submission:** You submit the complaint via Live Chat, email, phone, or the Help Center portal.
2. **Case Creation:** A unique case ID is generated and emailed to you within 15 minutes of submission.
3. **Initial Review:** A senior agent reads all prior interactions and account notes related to the issue.
4. **Investigation:** The relevant specialist team conducts an internal review of the situation.
5. **Resolution Offer:** A proposed resolution is communicated to you and requires your acknowledgment.
6. **Outcome Implementation:** The agreed resolution is applied — this may be a refund, account correction, policy exception, or formal acknowledgment.
7. **Case Closure:** You receive a summary email and a short satisfaction survey.

### Types of Resolutions We Offer
Depending on the nature of the complaint, our team can provide financial compensation such as a refund, fee waiver, or account credit; service recovery such as a replacement product or expedited reshipping; a policy exception such as an extended return window or one-time override; or a formal acknowledgment including an apology letter and confirmation of any internal coaching actions taken.

---

## Section 5: Response Time Commitments

Our team operates on four priority levels. Each level carries a defined initial response time and a resolution target.

**Critical** — applies to safety concerns, data breaches, and platform outages. Initial response within 1 hour; resolution targeted within 24 hours.

**High** — applies to financial loss, account lockout, and undelivered orders. Initial response within 4 business hours; resolution targeted within 2 business days.

**Medium** — applies to service dissatisfaction and minor billing discrepancies. Initial response within 8 business hours; resolution targeted within 3–5 business days.

**Low** — applies to general feedback and non-urgent cosmetic issues. Initial response within 24 business hours; resolution targeted within 7 business days.

---

## Section 6: External Escalation Options

If our internal process does not produce a satisfactory outcome after 30 days, you have the right to escalate to external bodies.

In the United States, you may file a complaint with the **Better Business Bureau** at bbb.org, or initiate a chargeback through your credit card issuer for unresolved billing disputes.

EU residents may escalate privacy-related complaints to their national **Data Protection Authority** under GDPR rights.

For general consumer protection matters, contact the consumer protection agency in your country or region.

Filing externally does not close your internal case with us — we remain engaged in parallel and cooperate fully with all legitimate regulatory inquiries.

---

## Section 7: Feedback After Resolution

When your escalation is closed, you will receive a three-question survey asking whether your issue was resolved, how you would rate the team's professionalism on a scale of 1 to 5, and whether you have any additional comments.

Your responses are reviewed by the Customer Experience Manager. Cases with low satisfaction scores are flagged for internal review within 48 hours, and findings are used to improve agent training and update support policies.
