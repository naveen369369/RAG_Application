# Shipping & Delivery Information — Customer Help Center

## Overview

From the moment you complete checkout to the moment your package arrives, every step is tracked and managed. This guide covers our available shipping methods, how order processing works before dispatch, how to track your shipment, and what to do if your package is delayed, damaged, or marked delivered before it arrives. We ship to over 80 countries and process orders on business days, Monday through Friday.

---

## Section 1: Shipping Methods and Costs

Choose the option that fits your timeline and budget at checkout.

Standard Shipping:5–7 business days, $4.99, order cutoff 3:00 PM EST.
Free Standard (orders $50+):5–7 business days, free, order cutoff 3:00 PM EST.
Expedited Shipping:2–3 business days, $9.99, order cutoff 3:00 PM EST.
Overnight / Next-Day:1 business day, $24.99, order cutoff 12:00 PM EST.
Same-Day Delivery:Same business day, $14.99, order cutoff 10:00 AM local.

Same-Day Delivery is only available in select metro areas. Enter your zip code at checkout to see if it is available at your address.

For international orders, delivery timelines range from 5 to 25 business days depending on your region, with shipping costs starting at $12.99. International shipments may be subject to customs duties and import taxes, which are the customer's responsibility. Delays at customs are outside our control but our team will assist if clearance takes more than five business days.

## Section 2: Order Processing Before Dispatch

Understanding the difference between processing time and shipping time prevents confusion about when to expect your package.

After your payment is confirmed, our warehouse team picks, packs, and performs a quality check on your order. This takes 1–2 business days. At the end of the second business day, the package is handed to the carrier and a tracking number is emailed to you.

Orders placed after the daily cutoff time are queued for the next business day. Orders submitted on weekends or public holidays begin processing the following Monday morning, or the next available business day. If your order contains multiple items, they may be packed and dispatched separately depending on warehouse availability — each shipment gets its own tracking number.

## Section 3: Tracking Your Order

Once your tracking number is emailed to you, you can follow your shipment in two ways.

The easiest method is through your account: go to **Orders > Order History**, select your order, and click **Track Shipment**. This takes you directly to the live carrier tracking page.

Alternatively, copy the tracking number from your shipping confirmation email and enter it manually on the carrier's website. We use UPS for expedited and overnight domestic orders, FedEx for overnight and international shipments, USPS for standard domestic delivery, and DHL for most international routes. Same-day delivery is handled by a local courier, and a tracking link specific to that carrier will be included in your confirmation email.

### Understanding Your Tracking Statuses
- **Order Received** — payment was confirmed and processing has not yet started.
- **Processing** — the warehouse is preparing your shipment.
- **Shipped** — the package has been handed to the carrier.
- **Out for Delivery** — the package is on the delivery vehicle today.
- **Delivered** — the carrier has marked the package as delivered.
- **Delivery Attempted** — the carrier tried to deliver but could not; a notice was left.
- **Exception** — an unexpected delay occurred; contact our support team for details.

## Section 4: What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

### Package Marked Delivered but Not Received
Carriers occasionally mark a package as delivered up to one day before it physically arrives. Wait 24 hours before taking action. During that time, check with neighbors, look in your building's mailroom or package lockers, and look for any delivery notice card left by the carrier. If the package still has not appeared after 24 hours, contact our support team with your order number and tracking number. We will open a carrier investigation within one business day. If the investigation confirms the package was lost, we will issue either a replacement or a full refund within 5 business days — your choice.

### Package Arrived Damaged
Do not discard the original packaging — it is required for the carrier damage claim. Take clear photos of both the damaged box and the item inside, then contact our support team within 48 hours of delivery. Share the photos along with your order number. Once confirmed, a replacement ships within 2 business days.

### Shipment Delayed
Weather-related delays are handled directly by the carrier and usually resolve without action needed on your part — your tracking will update automatically. For international orders delayed at customs beyond 5 days, our team will contact the customs authority on your behalf. If your package appears lost in transit or the tracking has not updated in more than 5 business days, contact us so we can open an investigation with the carrier.

## Section 5: Changing Your Order After Checkout

Address changes and cancellations are only possible while the order is still in the processing stage, before a tracking number has been generated.

To change your delivery address, contact our Live Chat team immediately with your order number and the correct address. To upgrade or downgrade your shipping method, contact us and we will apply the price difference as either a charge or a refund. To cancel the order entirely, go to **Orders > Order History** and click **Cancel Order** while the status still shows Processing.

Once a tracking number is generated and the package is with the carrier, we cannot make changes on our end. In that case, contact the carrier directly using your tracking number to request a package hold or address redirect — availability and fees depend on the carrier's own policies.
