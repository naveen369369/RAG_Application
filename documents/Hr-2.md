# Billing & Payment Support — Customer Help Center

## Overview

Understanding your bill and resolving payment issues quickly is a core priority for our team. This guide covers how payments are processed, what your invoice contains, how to handle common billing problems, and how to dispute a charge if something looks incorrect. Standard billing inquiries are handled within one business day; premium members receive a response within two hours.

---

## Section 1: Accepted Payment Methods

We support multiple payment options to make checkout easy regardless of your location or preference.

- **Credit and Debit Cards:** Visa, Mastercard, and American Express are accepted in USD, EUR, GBP, and INR. Transactions process instantly with no fee.
- **PayPal:** Accepted in USD, EUR, and GBP. Transactions are instant with no additional fee.
- **Bank Transfer (ACH):** Available for USD only. Transfers take 2–3 business days to clear.
- **UPI:** Available for customers in India only. Transactions process instantly.
- **Cryptocurrency:** Bitcoin and Ethereum are accepted at USD equivalent value. Processing takes 10–30 minutes and carries a 1% network fee.
- **Gift Cards and Store Credit:** Applied at checkout instantly in platform currency.

> Prepaid debit cards are not recommended for subscription payments — they are frequently declined by recurring billing systems. Use a standard credit or debit card for subscriptions.

## Section 2: Understanding Your Invoice

Each invoice issued by our platform contains the same structure, making it easy to verify every charge.

### Invoice Header
The header displays your **Invoice Number** (e.g., INV-2026-00423), the **Billing Date** on which the charge was processed, and the **Due Date** for accounts on net-30 terms (30 days after the billing date).

### Line Items
Every charge is listed individually. Your invoice will show the item name, a short description, quantity, unit price, and the line total. Applicable taxes are calculated based on your billing address and listed as a separate line below the subtotal.

### Downloading an Invoice
Go to **Billing > Invoice History**, find the relevant invoice, and click the **Download PDF** icon. Invoices remain available in your account history for 24 months.

## Section 3: Common Billing Issues and Resolutions

Two charges on the same date: Bank authorization hold alongside actual charge. The hold clears in 3–5 days; contact your bank if both persist after that.
Unexpected charge after a free trial: Auto-renewal was not cancelled before trial ended. Request a refund within the 7-day post-charge window.
Subscription active but payment not reflected:Payment gateway processing delay. Wait 24 hours; share your payment receipt if the issue continues.
-Payment declined — insufficient funds: Card balance too low at time of charge. Add a new payment method under Account > Payment Methods.
Payment declined — expired card:Stored card has passed its expiry date. Add a valid replacement card in your account settings.
Incorrect amount charged:Promo code not applied at checkout. Contact support with the promo code; a billing adjustment will be issued.

## Section 4: Refund Eligibility

Refunds are reviewed against the following criteria before approval.

- **Subscription plans:** A full refund is available within 7 days of purchase if the service was not used.
- **One-time purchases:** Eligible for a refund within 14 days if the item or service was not delivered.
- **Partial refunds:** A pro-rated refund is issued when a monthly plan is cancelled early.
- **Non-refundable:** Gift cards, cryptocurrency transactions, and payment processing fees cannot be refunded under any circumstance.

Once a refund is approved, it processes to your original payment method. Credit and debit card refunds take 5–10 business days. PayPal refunds arrive in 1–3 business days. ACH bank refunds take 5–7 business days. If you need faster access to your funds, request a store credit refund instead — it applies to your account within 24 hours.

## Section 5: Disputing a Charge

If you believe a charge is unauthorized, duplicated, or incorrect, use our internal dispute process before contacting your bank.

### How to File an Internal Dispute
1. Go to **Billing > Transaction History**.
2. Click **Dispute This Charge** next to the transaction in question.
3. Select a reason from the dropdown — options include Unauthorized, Duplicate, and Incorrect Amount.
4. Write a brief description of the issue (150 words maximum).
5. Submit the form. Our billing team will review and respond within 2 business days.

### Why Not to File a Bank Chargeback First
Initiating a chargeback directly with your bank before contacting us may result in a temporary account suspension while the case is under investigation. If the dispute is resolved in our favor, a $25 chargeback processing fee is applied to your account. The internal dispute route is faster, free, and does not put your account at risk.
