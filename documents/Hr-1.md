# Account Management & Login Issues — Customer Help Center

## Overview

Account access is the foundation of everything you do with us. Whether you are signing in for the first time, recovering a forgotten password, or managing your profile details, this guide walks you through every step. Our support team resolves over 4,000 account-related cases every month, and the guidance here captures the most effective, tested resolutions.

---

## Section 1: Common Login Problems and Quick Fixes

Invalid credentials error:Wrong password or email typo. Check Caps Lock and re-enter credentials carefully.
Account locked after 5 attempts: Security lockout policy triggered. Wait 30 minutes or use "Forgot Password".
Page keeps loading with no login:Browser cache or cookies conflict. Clear cache or switch to incognito mode.
Account not found message:Email not registered or contains typo. Check your registration confirmation email.
Two-factor code not arriving:Incorrect phone number on file. Contact support to verify and update your number.
SSO login fails (Google / Microsoft):Token expired or permission revoked. Re-authorize the connected app in your browser.

## Section 2: Step-by-Step Password Reset Guide

If you have forgotten your password or suspect it was compromised, follow these four steps to regain access.

### Step 1 — Request a Reset Link
Go to the login page and click **Forgot Password**. Enter the email address linked to your account and click **Send Reset Link**.

### Step 2 — Check Your Inbox
The reset email arrives within 2 minutes under normal conditions. If you do not see it, check your **Spam** or **Promotions** how folder. The reset link is valid for **60 minutes** only — request a new one if it expires.

### Step 3 — Create a New Password
Your new password must be **8–20 characters** long and include at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character such as @, #, or $. It cannot match any of your last three passwords.

### Step 4 — Log In with New Credentials
After saving your password you will be redirected to the login page automatically. Use your updated credentials to sign in.

## Section 3: Account Security Best Practices

Protecting your account prevents unauthorized access to your personal data, order history, and saved payment methods.

- **Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):** Set this up via SMS or an Authenticator App. It blocks over 99% of automated account attacks. Go to **My Account > Security > Two-Factor Authentication**.
- **Turn on Login Notifications:** You will receive an email each time a new login is detected. This lets you act quickly if someone else signs in.
- **Set a Session Timeout:** A 30-minute inactivity timeout reduces risk on shared or public devices. Configure this under **My Account > Security Settings**.
- **Register Trusted Devices:** Add up to three personal devices to reduce 2FA prompts on machines only you use.
- **Keep Your Recovery Email Current:** An outdated recovery email will block you from regaining access if your primary email is also compromised.

> Never share your password or 2FA code with any support agent, including ours. Legitimate support never asks for these.

## Section 4: Managing Your Account Profile

### Updating Personal Information
Name, address, and phone number can be updated at any time under **My Account > Profile Settings**. Changes to your email address require an email verification step — you will receive a confirmation link at the new address before the change takes effect.

### Linked Payment Methods
Up to five cards can be saved under **My Account > Payment Methods**. Removing a card does not automatically cancel subscriptions linked to it. Update the payment source for each active subscription separately to avoid interruption.

### Profile Photo
Accepted formats are JPEG and PNG. The file must be under 2 MB. Your photo is visible to support agents when you open a ticket.

## Section 5: Account Deactivation and Deletion

These are two distinct actions with very different consequences.

**Deactivation** hides your account from other users and pauses all activity, but your data is retained for 90 days. You can reactivate at any time simply by logging back in.

**Permanent Deletion** removes all your data, order history, and saved preferences after a 30-day grace period. This action cannot be reversed once the grace period ends. If you are unsure, always choose deactivation first.

To request either action, go to **My Account > Account Settings > Close Account** or contact our support team directly.

## Section 6: When to Contact Support

Attempt self-service steps first. Reach out to our team if you encounter any of the following situations:

- Your account shows purchases or activity you did not authorize.
- You are not receiving reset emails even after checking your spam folder.
- Your account was deactivated or suspended without any action on your part.
- You believe your account has been accessed by someone else.
