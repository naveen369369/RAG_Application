# 12 Golden Questions & Answers — Customer Help Center

---

## Article Type-1: Account Management & Login Issues

**Question 1**
What should I do if my account gets locked after multiple failed login attempts?

**Answer 1**
If your account is locked due to 5 failed login attempts, you have two options: wait 30 minutes for the lockout to lift automatically, or use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset your credentials immediately. After clicking "Forgot Password," enter your registered email address, check your inbox for the reset link (also check Spam/Promotions), and use the link within 60 minutes to set a new password. Your new password must be 8–20 characters and include at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character.

---

**Question 2**
What is the difference between account deactivation and permanent account deletion?

**Answer 2**
Deactivation hides your account while retaining all your data for 90 days, and you can reactivate simply by logging back in during that period. Permanent deletion, on the other hand, removes all your data after a 30-day grace period and cannot be undone once that window passes. If you are unsure, choose deactivation first — it gives you time to reconsider without losing your account history, orders, or settings.

## Article Type-2: Billing & Payment Support

**Question 3**
Why do I see two charges on my bank statement for the same order?

**Answer 3**
Two charges on the same date are typically the result of a temporary bank authorization hold (placed when you submit the order) appearing alongside the actual charge once the order is confirmed. The authorization hold clears automatically within 3–5 business days and is not a real deduction. If both charges remain after 5 business days, contact your bank first to confirm; if both are actual debits, contact our billing support team via Live Chat with your order number and transaction IDs so we can investigate and issue a refund for the duplicate.

---

**Question 4**
How long does a refund take to appear after it is approved?

**Answer 4**
Refund timelines depend on the original payment method. Credit and debit card refunds take 5–10 business days to appear on your statement after approval. PayPal refunds process in 1–3 business days. Bank transfer (ACH) refunds take 5–7 business days. If you prefer a faster option, you can request the refund as store credit, which is applied to your account within 24 hours. You will receive an email confirmation the moment the refund is processed on our end.

## Article Type-3: Product Returns & Refund Policy

**Question 5**
Can I return an electronic item that I have already opened?

**Answer 5**
Yes, opened electronics can be returned within 30 days, but only if the item is defective or not functioning as described. If the item is working correctly but you simply changed your mind after opening it, it may not qualify for a return unless it was still in original, resalable condition with all accessories and packaging included. Non-defective opened electronics are reviewed on a case-by-case basis. To start the process, go to Orders > Order History, select the item, click Start Return, and choose the return reason — a support agent will review and confirm eligibility within 1 business day.

---

**Question 6**
What happens if I receive a damaged item? Do I need to ship it back?

**Answer 6**
No return shipping is required for items verified as damaged on arrival. Document the damage by taking 3–5 clear photos of both the packaging and the product, then contact our support team within 48 hours of delivery via Live Chat or email. Attach the photos to your support ticket along with your order number. Our team will confirm the damage and offer you a choice: a replacement shipped within 2 business days or a full refund. You keep the damaged item — no return label is needed.

## Article Type-4: Technical Troubleshooting Guide

**Question 7**
What does error code ERR-1001 mean and how do I fix it?

**Answer 7**
ERR-1001 means your authentication token has expired. This typically happens when you have been logged into the platform for an extended period without activity, or when your session was interrupted. The fix is straightforward: log out of your account completely, then log back in with your credentials. If you are using SSO (Google or Microsoft login), re-authorize the connected app. If you encounter ERR-1001 repeatedly even after fresh logins, clear your browser cookies and cache, then try again. If the problem persists, contact our technical support team with the time and date of the error.

---

**Question 8**
My app keeps crashing on mobile. What steps should I follow to fix it?

**Answer 8**
Follow these steps in order: First, force-close the app and relaunch it. Second, check for a pending app update in the App Store or Google Play — crashes are often caused by running an outdated version. Third, clear the app cache by going to your phone's Settings > Apps > [App Name] > Clear Cache. Fourth, if the crash continues, uninstall and reinstall the app. Fifth, if the crash only happens on a specific screen, note the exact steps that trigger it and report the issue via the in-app feedback option. If none of these steps resolve it, contact our Tier 2 Technical Specialist through Live Chat with your device model, OS version, and app version.

## Article Type-5: Shipping & Delivery Information

**Question 9**
My tracking status shows "Delivered" but I never received my package. What should I do?

**Answer 9**
First, wait 24 hours — carriers sometimes mark packages as delivered up to one day before they actually arrive. While waiting, check with neighbors, your building's mailroom, or any secure package lockers in your area, and look for a delivery notice that may indicate the package was left at a pickup point. If the package still has not arrived after 24 hours, contact our support team with your order number and tracking number. We will open a carrier investigation within 1 business day. If the investigation confirms the package was lost, we will issue either a replacement shipment or a full refund within 5 business days.

---

**Question 10**
Can I change the delivery address on my order after it has been placed?

**Answer 10**
Address changes are possible only while your order is still in the processing stage, before a tracking number has been generated. To request a change, contact our Live Chat support team immediately with your order number and the correct delivery address — agents can update the address in the system if the warehouse has not yet packed the shipment. Once a tracking number is assigned and the package has been handed to the carrier, the address cannot be changed on our end. In that case, you would need to contact the carrier directly (UPS, FedEx, USPS, or DHL) to request a package hold or address redirect, which is subject to the carrier's own policies and fees.

## Article Type-6: Customer Escalation & Complaint Resolution

**Question 11**
How do I escalate my complaint to a senior agent or manager?

**Answer 11**
There are three ways to request an escalation. During a Live Chat or phone call, simply say "I would like to escalate this to a senior agent" and the agent is required to transfer you or arrange a callback from a team lead. Via email, reply to your existing support ticket and include the phrase ESCALATION REQUEST in the subject line — this flags the case in our system for priority review. Through the Help Center portal, navigate to My Cases > Open Case > Request Escalation. Once escalated, a Senior Agent or Tier 2 Specialist will contact you within 4–8 business hours. For critical issues (such as a data breach or safety concern), escalation response time is within 1 hour.

---

**Question 12**
What are my options if the company's internal resolution does not satisfy my complaint?

**Answer 12**
If our internal resolution process does not meet your expectations after 30 days, you have several external options. In the US, you can file a complaint with the Better Business Bureau (BBB) at bbb.org or initiate a chargeback through your credit card issuer for unresolved billing disputes. EU residents can escalate privacy-related concerns to their national Data Protection Authority (DPA) under GDPR. For general consumer protection issues, you may contact the consumer protection agency in your region. Filing externally does not close your case with us — we remain committed to working toward resolution in parallel. We also actively cooperate with all legitimate regulatory inquiries.
