# Product Returns & Refund Policy — Customer Help Center

## Overview

We want every purchase to meet your expectations. If something is not right, our returns process is designed to be straightforward and fast. This guide explains which items are eligible for return, how to start the process, what happens once we receive your item, and how exchanges and damaged-item claims are handled. Our average return resolution time is 5 business days from the moment we receive your shipment.

---

## Section 1: Return Eligibility by Product Category

Not all items qualify for return. Check the list below before starting the return process to confirm your item is eligible.

Electronics:30-day return window. Must be unopened or verified defective. Original packaging must be included.
Clothing & Apparel:60-day return window. Must be unworn with original tags attached. Final-sale items are not returnable.
Home & Kitchen:30-day return window. Must be unused and undamaged. Assembled items are not accepted.
Digital Downloads:7-day return window. Must not yet be downloaded. One exception allowed per account lifetime.
Software Licenses:Non-refundable. Eligible only if the license key was never delivered.
Perishables & Food:Non-refundable. Eligible only if delivered in damaged condition.
Customized Items:Non-refundable. Eligible only if the item arrives defective.

Items that are never eligible for return regardless of condition include hazardous materials (such as batteries or flammable goods), anything marked **Final Sale** at the time of purchase, and products returned without a valid order number or receipt.

---

## Section 2: How to Start a Return

### Using the Online Return Portal
The fastest way to initiate a return is through your account.

1. Log in and go to **Orders > Order History**.
2. Select the order that contains the item you want to return.
3. Click **Start Return** next to the specific item.
4. Choose your return reason from the dropdown menu.
5. Select your preferred return method — **Drop-off** or **Scheduled Pickup**.
6. Download and print the prepaid return label, or have it sent to your email.
7. Pack the item securely and attach the label to the outside of the box.

> Do not ship the item before receiving your return authorization label. Packages sent without authorization may be refused at the warehouse.

### Using Customer Support
If you cannot access the online portal, contact us via Live Chat or phone. Provide your order number and the item name or SKU. An agent will email you a prepaid label within 30 minutes.

---

## Section 3: Return Shipping and Packaging

Return shipping is free for all eligible items. You can choose between dropping off the prepaid-label package at the nearest carrier location or scheduling a free pickup at your address. If you prefer to arrange your own shipping, the cost is your responsibility and the carrier and transit time are at your discretion.

When packing your return, use the original box whenever possible. Wrap fragile items with bubble wrap or foam. Include all accessories, manuals, and original inserts. Do not write or tape anything to the product's own box — place it inside an outer shipping box with the return label affixed to the outside.

---

## Section 4: What Happens After We Receive Your Return

Once your package arrives at our warehouse, the process moves through four stages:

1. **Item Received (Day 0):** Your tracking updates automatically to confirm warehouse receipt.
2. **Quality Inspection (Days 1–2):** Staff verify the item's condition against our eligibility criteria.
3. **Decision Notification (Day 2–3):** You receive an email confirming whether the refund was approved or denied, and the reason if denied.
4. **Refund Processed (Days 5–10):** Approved refunds are sent to your original payment method. Timeline varies by payment type.

### Refund Amount Outcomes
- **Full refund** — item is returned in original, undamaged condition with all original components.
- **Partial refund (up to 20% deducted)** — item shows signs of use but is otherwise acceptable for restocking.
- **Refund denied** — item is damaged beyond normal wear, missing parts, or was returned outside the eligible window.

## Section 5: Exchanges

If you want a different size, color, or model instead of a refund, you can request an exchange within the same return window.

### How to Request an Exchange
1. Start a return through the **Order History** portal.
2. When prompted to choose a resolution, select **Exchange** rather than Refund.
3. Choose your replacement item from the available options.
4. Your exchange ships as soon as the original item is received and inspected at the warehouse.

Exchanges for the same item in a different size or color ship within 1–2 business days of return receipt, subject to stock availability. If you want a different item at the same price, a store credit is issued and you place a new order. For an upgrade, an agent processes the price difference at the time of exchange.

---

## Section 6: Damaged or Defective Items on Arrival

If your order arrives damaged or the product does not function as described, a simplified claims process applies — no immediate return shipping is required.

1. Take 3–5 clear photos of the packaging and the damaged or defective product within 48 hours of delivery.
2. Contact our support team via Live Chat or email with your order number and attach the photos.
3. Our team will review the photos and confirm the issue.
4. You choose your preferred resolution: a replacement shipped within 2 business days, or a full refund.

Verified damaged or defective items do not need to be shipped back. The process is handled entirely through your photos and the support ticket.
